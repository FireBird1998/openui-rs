# 04 — Sketch three story screens

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: none

## Question

What should a story screen actually look like? Rough sketches, or programs rendered with the existing web renderer, of three contrasting stories:
- an earnings report
- an ongoing event that needs a timeline
- breaking news that needs a key-facts card

We react to them together to find out which components the news screens really need.
