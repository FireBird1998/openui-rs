# 03 — Which news sources can a personal showcase use?

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: none

## Question

Which news sources can a personal, non-commercial showcase app legally use (RSS feeds, free news APIs, public-broadcaster feeds)? For each, find out:
- whether it provides full text or a headline plus summary
- rate limits and cost
- licence terms on showing and transforming the content with an LLM
